\begin{problem}{2012}{G}{福建卷（理）}{2}{数列}
等差数列 $ \{a_n\} $ 中，$ a_1 + a_5 = 10 $，$ a_4 = 7 $，则数列 $ \{a_n\} $ 的公差为
\begin{choices}
\choice{{$1$}}
\choice{{$2$}}
\choice{{$3$}}
\choice{{$4$}}
\end{choices}
\end{problem}

\begin{answer}
(B)
\end{answer}

\begin{solutions}
设公差为 $ d $，则 $ a_1 + a_5 = a_1 + (a_1 + 4d) = 2a_1 + 4d = 10 $，即 $ a_1 + 2d = 5 $；又 $ a_4 = a_1 + 3d = 7 $.联立解得 $ d = 2 $.
\end{solutions}
