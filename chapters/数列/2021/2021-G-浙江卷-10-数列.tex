\begin{problem}{2021}{G}{浙江卷}{10}{数列}
已知数列 $\{a_n\}$ 满足 $a_1=1, a_{n+1}=\dfrac{a_n}{1+\sqrt[3]{a_n}}(n\in \mathbf{N}^*)$. 记数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $S_n$，则
(\hspace{1cm})
\begin{choices}
\choice{{$\dfrac{3}{2} < S_{100} < 3$}}
\choice{{$3 < S_{100} < 4$}}
\choice{{$4 < S_{100} < \dfrac{9}{2}$}}
\choice{{$\dfrac{9}{2} < S_{100} < 5$}}
\end{choices}
\end{problem}

\begin{answer}
A
\end{answer}

\begin{solutions}
首先将式子变型成更好处理的形式, 对两边取倒数:
$$
\dfrac{1}{a_{n+1}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{a_n}+1}{a_n}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}+\dfrac{1}{a_n}
$$
进一步变型并对两边取平方根, 可以得到第一个有用的放缩:
$$
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_{n+1}}}=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2-\dfrac{1}{4}}<\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}+\dfrac{1}{2} \quad \text{即} \quad \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_{n+1}}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}<\dfrac{1}{2}
$$
$$
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_2}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_1}}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_3}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_2}}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_{n-1}}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}-1<\dfrac{n-1}{2}
$$
即 $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{a_n}}\leqslant\dfrac{n+1}{2}$, $a_n\geqslant\dfrac{4}{(n+1)^2}$

稍微放缩一下便于裂项: $a_n\geqslant\dfrac{4}{(n+1)^2}>\dfrac{4}{(n+1)(n+2)}=4\left(\dfrac{1}{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{n+2}\right)$, 保留前两项以提高精度: $S_{100}>1+\dfrac{1}{2}+4\left(\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{5}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{101}-\dfrac{1}{102}\right)>\dfrac{5}{2}$, 不能选出或排除选项, 因此我们还要考虑 $S_{100}$ 的上界:
$$
\dfrac{a_n}{a_{n-1}}=\dfrac{1}{1+\sqrt{a_{n-1}}}\leqslant\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{2}{n}}=\dfrac{n}{n+2}
$$
$$
a_n=a_1\cdot\dfrac{a_2}{a_1}\cdot\dfrac{a_3}{a_2}\cdot\cdots\cdot\dfrac{a_n}{a_{n-1}}\leqslant1\cdot\dfrac{2}{2+2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{3+2}\cdot\cdots\cdot\dfrac{n}{n+2}=\dfrac{6}{(n+1)(n+2)}
$$
$$
S_{100}\leqslant6\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{101}-\dfrac{1}{102}\right)<3
$$
综上选 A.
\end{solutions}
